Porter Ranch Real Estate Market Update: September 2026

By Josh Stepakoff, Realtor | October 7, 2026

Every month I pull the closed sales and listing numbers for Porter Ranch so I can give my clients a straight read on the market. September's numbers are in, and they tell a pretty clear story: prices are holding steady, buyers have more to choose from, and homes are taking a little longer to sell than they did a year ago.

All figures below are for single-family homes in ZIP code 91326 for September 2026, from MLS data via Rechat. Numbers in a single ZIP can bounce around month to month because the sample is small, so I look at the trend more than any one data point.

The headline: prices are steady

The median sale price for a single-family home in 91326 was $1,200,000 in September. That is almost exactly where August landed ($1,199,000) and about 2% below September 2025, when the median was $1,225,000.

In plain terms, prices are flat. We are not seeing the big swings that make headlines. Earlier this year the monthly median jumped around more (it was $1,325,250 in June and $1,184,000 in July), but the last two months have settled right around $1.2 million.

Keep in mind that a median describes the market, not your house. Two homes on the same street can sell for very different prices depending on floor plan, condition, and lot. That is why I never price a home off a neighborhood median.

More homes for sale

This is the biggest change from a year ago. There were 58 active single-family listings in 91326 at the end of September, compared with 47 in September 2025. That is about 23% more inventory.

New listings picked up too. Sellers brought 22 new single-family homes to market in September, up from 17 in August and 19 a year ago.

More choice is good news for buyers. For sellers, it means your home is competing against more options, so presentation and pricing matter more than they did during the tighter markets of the last few years.

Homes are taking longer to sell

The median home spent 31 days on the market in September, up from 21 days both in August and in September 2025. Measured from list date to closing, the median was 62 days, compared with 55 days a year ago.

Pending sales also dipped to 6 at the end of the month, down from 13 in August. That is one number I will be watching closely in October, since pendings today become closed sales next month.

Well-priced homes still sell at full price

Here is the part I want sellers to hear. Homes that sold in September closed at a median of 100.4% of their list price. Buyers are still paying full price, and sometimes a little over, for homes that are priced right and show well.

There were 15 single-family sales in September, the same as August and up from 12 a year ago.

So the market is not slow. It is selective. The homes that sit are usually the ones that came out too high and had to chase the market down.

What this means if you are selling

With more inventory and longer days on market, the first two weeks of your listing matter more than ever. That is when the most buyers see your home, and when you get the strongest offers.

My advice right now:

  • Price it right from day one. Overpricing in a market with 58 competing homes is the fastest way to end up with a price reduction.
  • Fix the obvious things. Leaky faucets, scuffed paint, and tired landscaping are easy for buyers to notice and easy for you to fix.
  • Don't over-remodel before you list. Not every project pays you back. A very personal choice, like a bold color or a finish far from what buyers expect today, can shrink your buyer pool instead of growing it.

If you're thinking about selling in the next six months, I'm happy to walk your home and tell you exactly what I'd do and what I'd skip.

What this means if you are buying

This is the most room buyers in Porter Ranch have had in a while. You have more homes to compare, a bit more time to decide, and in some cases more room to negotiate on homes that have been sitting.

That said, the well-priced, move-in ready homes are still moving at full price. If you see one you love, be ready to act.

In September 2026 the median single-family home spent 31 days on the market, and about 62 days from listing to closing. A home that is priced correctly and prepped well can beat that. One that comes out too high usually takes longer.
